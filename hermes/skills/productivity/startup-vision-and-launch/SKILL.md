# Startup Vision & Launch (phase-gated consulting)

Help a founder/team that is "losing direction" or about to publish before they are ready. The trap is almost always the same: they want to build/publish before deciding WHAT the business is. Product is a vehicle, not the vision.

## Core ordering principle
NEVER start from branding/publishing. Sequence: ground truth -> business core -> customer & problem -> vision/mission/values -> positioning -> VALIDATE -> price -> MVP -> SOP -> soft launch -> publish -> measure. Each phase has a GATE; do not advance until it passes.

## Phase-gated roadmap (see references/phase_roadmap.md for full detail)
- Fase 0 Ground truth: money/runway, single decision-maker, what the company IS now, real time commitment, the uncomfortable truth. Gate: honesty, one boss, know the runway.
- Fase 1 Business inti: pick the PRIMARY revenue engine vs lead magnet vs long-term vision vehicle, and the relationship between them (funnel). Gate: one-line "we earn money from __".
- Fase 2 Customer & problem: name the beachhead (one target + root problem). If user answers "all customers / all problems", that is the SYMPTOM of no narrowing — but don't force choosing one symptom either: cluster them under ONE ROOT cause (e.g. four pains all stem from "data is messy/manual"). Gate: one customer + one root problem spoken in one breath.
- Fase 3 Vision/Mission/Values: vision = long-term North star (NO product names, NO prices). Mission = what you do now. Slogan is the PUBLIC short form of the vision — derive vision/mission from a good slogan for consistency. Gate: vision doesn't name the product.
- Fase 4 Positioning: one-liner "We are ___ for ___ so that ___". Slogan is the CLOSING tagline, not the opener. Gate: positioning not something competitors can say.
- Fase 5 Validation: 8-15 real interviews, screen for budget + decision-maker first, don't pitch/show product early, don't name price first. Gate: >=5 say "yes/would try" + name a price.
- Fase 6 Pricing: price by VALUE not cost. Compute cost incl. hidden support time — a price that equals VPS/API cost is a loss once you add support hours + overhead. Gate: written packages, positive margin, a free entry tier.
- Fase 7 MVP: smallest thing that makes the client FEEL value & pay, not the smallest-full-featured thing. Explicit MUST vs DEFER lists; scope lock; "extra feature = delay launch".
- Fase 8 Internal SOP: roles & owners per stage, sales pipeline (stages + deadline), consultation SOP, onboarding SOP, reporting templates.
- Fase 9-11: soft launch/beta -> public publish -> measure (leads/consults/deals, kill what fails in 30d).

## Pitfalls learned (encode these)
- A free "consultation/assessment" is now the NORM in the AI-agency market — it is a ticket, not a differentiator. Differentiate AFTER it (measured readiness score, vertical focus, a recurring product).
- Pricing to your own cost ("250k = VPS+API") is a false idea of profit: include support TIME + overhead; margin often thin or negative. Price by value delivered (hours saved, loss prevented).
- "Semua bisnis / semua masalah" = the team has not narrowed. But don't force ONE problem either — cluster symptoms under a single root cause.
- Validate before publish. A ~3-month runway means validation & first revenue beat content/vanity publishing.
- Give the client real value in the free step (a scored readiness report + roadmap), not just a sales call — that separates free offers that are "basi" from ones that build trust.
