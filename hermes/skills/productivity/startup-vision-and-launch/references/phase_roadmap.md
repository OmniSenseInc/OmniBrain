# Phase-gated startup roadmap (detail)

Sequence with GATES. Do not advance past a phase whose gate fails.

## Fase 0 — Ground truth (1-3 days)
Get honest facts about the CURRENT state, not aspirations.
Questions: monthly money in/out and runway without income; single final decision-maker (one name, not "everyone agrees"); what the company IS now (consulting vs product vs concept); real hours/week per person; commitment deadline; the one uncomfortable truth being deferred.
OUTPUT: a worksheet the founder fills. GATE: every field filled honestly, one named boss, known runway.

## Fase 1 — Business core
Distinguish three roles and assign exactly one each: (A) primary revenue engine, (B) lead magnet / front door, (C) long-term vision vehicle / productized subscription. Decide the relationship (usually a funnel). For an AI agency: free readiness assessment (B) -> paid build/install (A/C). GATE: one line "OmniSense earns money from __".

## Fase 2 — Customer & problem
Name ONE beachhead target + ONE ROOT problem. If the founder says "all customers / all problems", that means narrowing hasn't happened — but DON'T force picking one symptom either. Cluster the symptoms under a single root cause (e.g. four pains all come from "data is messy/manual") so messaging has one center of gravity without dropping scope. GATE: one customer + one root problem spoken in one breath.

## Fase 3 — Vision/Mission/Values/Slogan
Vision = long-term North star, NO product names, NO prices (if vision names the product it's a product vision, wrong category). Mission = what you do NOW. Values = 3-4 behavior rules. If the founder has a slogan, derive vision/mission from it for consistency (slogan = public short form of vision). GATE: vision doesn't name a product.

## Fase 4 — Positioning
One-liner "We are ___ for ___ so that ___". Slogan sits as the CLOSING tagline, not the opener. Verify against competitors: if a generalist competitor could say the same line, sharpen it (measured score + vertical + recurring product are hard-to-copy). GATE: positioning not speak-eable by a competitor verbatim.

## Fase 5 — Validation (do NOT skip before publish)
8-15 real interviews with target buyers. Screen first (1 min): has budget + named decision-maker with authority. Protocol 4 stages: open/probe (how they work now, what wastes time), measure problem (which of the symptom cluster most), test value (which benefit attracts, ask them to NAME a price, don't lead), commitment (would you take it this month; if no, why). Record a scorecard per interviewee. Never pitch/show product early; never quote your price first. GATE: >=5 "yes / would try" AND they named a plausible price. If fewer, reframe problem/price and repeat before building further.

## Fase 6 — Pricing
Price by VALUE, not cost. Per-client cost = direct infra + API + support TIME + allocated overhead. A price that merely equals VPS/API is a loss once support hours + overhead are added. Three packages: free entry (lead magnet), a standard product subscription, a custom build/project tier. Decide prices from (a) cost+margin floor (2-4x) or (b) value metric (owner hours saved + loss prevented). GATE: written package prices, positive margin in the calc, a free tier, sell VALUE (profit, stock safety, time) not tech.

## Fase 7 — MVP
Smallest thing that makes the client FEEL value & pay, NOT a minified feature list. Explicit MUST (definition of done) vs DEFER lists; lock scope; "extra feature = delay launch". Tie MVP scope to the cheapest package tier. GATE: must-list fixed, defer-list excluded, everyone agrees (no silent gold-plating).

## Fase 8 — Internal SOP
Roles & owner per funnel stage; sales pipeline P1..Pn with per-stage deadlines (e.g. qualified->consult <1wk, proposal <3d after report); standard consultation session (open->measure->score->close without selling); onboarding flow; support/margin tracking. Create report/proposal templates. GATE: owners agreed, pipeline in use, consultation + onboarding SOP in place, templates exist.

## Fase 9-11 — Soft launch -> Publish -> Measure
Phase 9: 5-10 pilot customers, iterate on real feedback. Phase 10 (ONLY after validation): landing page (one promise, one CTA), business channels, value-based content (industry data), slogan as close. Phase 11: track leads / free consults / deals / churn; kill what doesn't work within ~30 days.

## Decision-makers in a 3-person startup
Map each funnel stage to an owner (e.g. CMO=inbound/qualify, CEO=deals/strategic, CTO=build/support). Have ONE final decision maker (CEO). When co-leading an interview/consult, assign roles (one handles value/closing, other handles needs/rapport) so the founder isn't confused by two voices.
