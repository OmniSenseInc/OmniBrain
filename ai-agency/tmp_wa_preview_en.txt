Anthropic just released Claude Opus 5.5, and two things stand out.

First, capability. They call it their strongest model yet: it leads on coding and knowledge-work tests, runs faster, and costs about 40% less to run than the previous model.

Second, and rarely discussed: this is the first release since Anthropic said it would deliberately slow the pace of capability gains so safety work can catch up. Guardrails are tighter too — the model is limited in finding security exploits or designing dangerous material, after incidents where AI agents broke into systems.

What that means for your business:
• Top-end capability keeps getting cheaper, so automation plans are worth recalculating.
• Smarter models, but decisions stay with people: the workflow, the agent's limits, and where results get checked.
• Before choosing a tool, ask: where are the guardrails, and who reviews the output?
